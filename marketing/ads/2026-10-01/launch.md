# Launch sheet, spring set

Five ads in a **new campaign with its own budget**, not a new ad set inside the September
campaign. That campaign shares one budget across its ad sets, so a spring ad set added there
would be starved the way terminal, price and bookietips were.

- **Objective:** Sales, optimised for the website `CompleteRegistration` event, same as September
- **Budget:** $10 to $15 a day, 5 to 7 days
- **Audience:** Australia, 18+, Advantage+ audience, no interests
- **Display link:** `theoverlay.com.au`
- **CTA:** Learn more
- **Media:** the three sizes from `marketing/ads/2026-10-01/spring/`, assigned with *Edit per placement*
  (`-portrait` to feed, `-story` to Stories and Reels, `-square` to the rest)

Every screenshot is from Illawarra Grange R8 on 1 Oct, shot before the jump. No bookie is named
anywhere: the price boxes read Market and the "at Sportsbet" links are gone.

The September pass and mate ads keep running as they are.

## Reading it

Meta counted 4 of the 8 September signups, so judge this set on our own records: signups and
trials in `profiles` with `utm.campaign = spring`. The September mark to beat is $16 a signup
and $22 a trial.

Meta will again put most of the money behind one or two ads in the first day. That is fine
inside one argument (spring), but note which ones never got 1,000 impressions before calling
them losers.

## What we cannot say

Same rules as September: no profit, ROI or strike-rate claim, no bookie named, and no betting
words in the copy ("bet", "punt", "odds"). springCrowd was "Everyone bets in spring" and is now
"Everyone has a go in spring". The screenshots are scrubbed too (`scripts/capture-ui.ts`):
the call badge reads Pick, "odds" reads "price", and the back edge, lay at, P/L and unit total
columns are taken out. If an ad is rejected anyway, request a review before rewording.
