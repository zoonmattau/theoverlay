# App set

Four concepts, each at 1:1 (`square`), 4:5 (`portrait`) and 9:16 (`story`). Twelve images.

This is the set for someone who has never heard of us. Every ad carries the mechanic
(`01 / 02 / 03`, or the one-line version of it), the benefit line and the price, so a
viewer who sees one of these and nothing else knows what the thing is and what it costs.

Five headlines, five primary texts and five descriptions each. Load all five of each into
the ad and let Meta find the pair that works. The headline burned into the artwork is the
concept's own line; to test another in the image, edit that concept's `h1` in
`scripts/meta-ads.ts` and re-render.

**CTA for all four:** Learn more. Sign up converts worse cold, because nobody signs up to
a $49 product from an ad they have seen once.

## Running this set

**Start with `terminal`.** It is the one that explains the product fastest, and the board
reads at thumbnail size where a table does not. `everyrace` is the same picture with the
coverage argument, so run it as the variant rather than alongside at equal spend.

**`tipsters` and `tipsterRecord` are a pair.** The first says what a tipster post looks
like, the second says you can check whether they are any good. Retarget with the second
after the first, rather than running both cold.

**Placements:** portrait carries feed, story for reels and stories. Square is the one to cut
if budget is tight; portrait outperforms it in feed for the same creative.

## Two things to fix before spend

**The tipster ads show Matt's page**, so they advertise your own tipster account rather than
the feature. If the pitch is "tipsters on The Overlay", re-shoot against a page listing
several. Say the word and I will.

**Meta has to authorise the ad account for gambling and betting ads** in Australia before
any of these will serve. That approval is the long pole, not the creative.

## What we cannot say

No ad in this set claims a profit, an ROI or a strike rate, and none should. The settled
ledger is 449 calls at **-6.5%**, bets alone at **-16.0%**. Every claim here is about
coverage, method or transparency, each true today and checkable by anyone who clicks.
