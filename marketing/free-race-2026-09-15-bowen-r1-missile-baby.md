# Claude Design prompt: free race of the day, Bowen R1, Missile Baby, Tue 15 Sep 2026

Paste everything below the line into Claude Design. For another day, copy this file with the new date and swap the race block at the bottom.

---

You are designing a two slide Instagram carousel for The Overlay (theoverlay.com.au), an Australian horse racing ratings service. The model rates every runner in benchmark points, split into early, mid and late sectionals, and turns that into a price. When the market is longer than our price it is a bet. One race a day is free for everyone. Slide one advertises that race and our Prime Overlay in it, slide two shows the rest of the field and their sectionals so the case is plain.

Brand system, use it exactly.
- Page #f3f4f0, panels #ffffff, ink #14161a, secondary ink #454a44, soft ink #6b716a, rules #dfe3db.
- Graphite bar #14161a with paper text #f5f7f2. Lime #c6f24e is the wordmark and the Prime Overlay fill. Text on white uses the darker lime #6f9a12.
- Lime is the Prime Overlay colour. Use it as a solid chip or panel fill, never as decoration.
- Type: Archivo for everything, IBM Plex Mono for prices and numbers.
- Radius 8px, one soft shadow, no gradients, no emoji, no stock photos, no horse illustrations.
- Copy is terse. One sentence explanations. Never an em dash. Prices are written $2.45 style.
- Wordmark top left, theoverlay.com.au bottom right, small.
- Show only our rated price, the market price and the edge.

Formats. 1080 x 1350 feed and 1080 x 1920 story, 80px safe margins. Same content, the story stacks taller.

Slide one, top to bottom.
1. Graphite bar with the wordmark and a lime chip reading FREE RACE.
2. Eyebrow: track, race number, distance, going, jump time, date.
3. The Prime Overlay panel, filled lime, the biggest thing on the page: saddlecloth number in a dark square, horse name huge, jockey and barrier under it, then two figures side by side in mono, "Market $2.45" and "Our price $2.25", with a PRIME OVERLAY chip and the edge.
4. One line under the panel on why, soft ink.
5. Footer call to action: "This race is free today at theoverlay.com.au. Every other race is for members."

Slide two, top to bottom.
1. Same graphite bar and FREE RACE chip, same eyebrow.
2. Heading: "The field on sectionals."
3. A table of every runner, sorted by Late, best first. Columns: saddlecloth number in a dark square, horse and jockey, Settles, Early, Mid, Late, Rated, Market. Sectional columns are our ratings in points, mono, one decimal, tabular. The best figure in each sectional column is bold, the Late column is the point of the slide so give it the most weight. Missile Baby's row is filled lime with a PRIME chip. Every other row is plain.
4. One line under the table, soft ink: "Late is the last 600. Nothing else in the race goes within 13 points of it."
5. Footer: "Free today at theoverlay.com.au."

Caption, three short lines, no hashtags in the graphic: the free race, the Prime Overlay with both prices, where to see it.

Rules on the data. Use only the details below. Do not invent a horse, a price or a finish.
