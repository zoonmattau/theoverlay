# Claude Design prompt: The Overlay season graphics

Paste everything below the line into Claude Design, then paste the contents of `season-record.md` after it as the data.

---

You are designing a set of Instagram graphics for The Overlay (theoverlay.com.au), an Australian horse racing ratings service. The model rates every runner in benchmark points and turns that into a price. When the market is longer than our price it is a bet, when the market is shorter it is a lay. The product is a race board: a dark graphite top bar with a lime wordmark, an off-white page, white panels, and a runner table with big saddlecloth style numbers, the horse name, our rated price against the market price, and a coloured chip when there is a call.

Brand system, use it exactly.
- Page #f3f4f0, panels #ffffff, ink #14161a, secondary ink #454a44, soft ink #6b716a, rules #dfe3db.
- Graphite bar #14161a with paper text #f5f7f2. Lime #c6f24e is the wordmark and the Prime Overlay fill. Text on white uses the darker lime #6f9a12.
- Call colours never change: lime = Prime Overlay, blue #1f6fd6 = bet, red #d93636 = lay. Use them as solid chips or cell fills, never as decoration.
- Type: Archivo for everything, IBM Plex Mono for prices, ratings and numbers. Tabular numerals so prices line up.
- Radius 8px, one soft shadow, no gradients, no emoji, no stock photos, no horse illustrations.
- Copy is terse. One sentence explanations. Never an em dash. Prices are written $4.80 style, or $4.8 is fine. Positions are 1st, 2nd, 3rd. Rated #1 means our top pick.
- Every graphic carries the wordmark top left and theoverlay.com.au bottom right, small.
- Rated prices are ours. Never show anyone else's ratings or a benchmark figure, only our rated price, the market price and the edge in points.

Formats. Make each template at 1080 x 1350 (feed) and 1080 x 1920 (story). Keep 80px safe margins.

Templates to build, each as one artboard I can duplicate and fill from the data.

1. Race preview, "The board". Eyebrow: grade, race name, track, distance, going, prize, date. Body: our top four as a runner table. Columns: rank, saddlecloth number in a dark square, horse and jockey, Rated, Market, then a call chip (BET blue, PRIME lime, LAY red) or nothing. Any bet or lay outside the top four goes in one row under the table headed "Also". Footer: the one line verdict. Story version is the same table stacked taller.

2. Race review, "How it ran". Same header. A result strip: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th with SP. Under it the top four table again with a Finished column, the winner row filled lime when it was our #1, blue when it was in our top four, plain when it was not. One line: "Winner was our #3 at $8." Calls settle with units: WON +4.00u, or 5th, minus 1u, lay held +1u.

3. Saturday wrap. Big number tile: "8 of 8" winners in our top four, then a compact list of every race that day, one row each: race, our rank of the winner, its price. Bets and lays for the day with units.

4. Group 1 spotlight. Square 1080 x 1080 too. The race name huge, then the winner, its rank and rated price against SP. Four of these: Winx Stakes, Memsie Stakes, Moir Stakes, Makybe Diva Stakes.

5. Season scoreboard. Winner in our top four 33 of 40 (83%), rated #1 won 10 of 40, Group 1 winners in our top four 4 of 4, quinella inside our top four 22 of 40. Then the honest line: bets 2 from 20 at level stakes, minus 7.2 units, lays 4 from 6 held, plus 0.9 units. Put the top four record first and biggest, the bet record smaller but present.

6. Story teaser, a countdown style card: "Saturday's board is up", the race and jump time, blurred table, "Free race every day" as the call to action.

Rules on the data. Use only the prices and results in the record. Do not invent a horse, a price or a finish. Where a race has no call, the preview shows the top four and nothing else. Dates run Sat 1 Aug 2026 to Sat 12 Sep 2026. A caption line for each post is welcome, one sentence, plain, no hashtags in the graphic.

Start with the two P.B. Lawrence Stakes cards (preview and review, Caulfield, 15 Aug: our top three ran the trifecta in order) and the Makybe Diva Stakes pair (Flemington, 12 Sep: rated #1 Sheza Alibi won at $1.6), so I can check the look before the rest.
